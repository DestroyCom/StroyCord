More `/config` settings. A bot replies channel, where the bot posts its messages instead of the channel of the command (errors stay in the command channel). A switch to turn off the bot replies (now playing, added, skip, pause…), the queue, current song and errors are still shown. A command mode to accept prefix commands, slash commands or both (`/config` and `/stats` always stay available). Blocked roles: members with these roles get no answer at all, admins keep access to `/config`.
